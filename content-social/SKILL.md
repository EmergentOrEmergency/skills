# Content Social

Create content that earns qualified attention and moves readers to a defined next step. Start from the approved book promise, audience, brand voice, claims, spoilers, channels, cadence, and conversion destination.

## Content system

Choose 3-5 durable pillars tied to reader interest, not merely announcements. For each pillar define its job, proof source, formats, calls to action, and prohibited angles. Balance discovery, trust, product relevance, and conversion; do not make every post a direct sale.

Create a source asset once, then adapt rather than copy-paste. Preserve meaning while changing hook, length, structure, aspect ratio, caption, tags, link handling, and interaction style for each platform. Do not claim universal best times; use account data when available and label generic timing as a test.

Respect author voice and factual boundaries. Do not fabricate reader quotes, endorsements, urgency, sales figures, or personal anecdotes. Mark AI-generated or synthetic media when law, platform rules, or audience expectations require it. Avoid copyrighted text or imagery without permission.

## Calendar and payloads

Every calendar item should include ID, date/time/timezone, channel, pillar, objective, audience, hook, body or script, creative brief, CTA, destination/UTM, dependencies, approval state, and experiment ID. Treat content as a draft until the connected tool confirms scheduling or publication.

Before external publication, preview exact text, media, destination, account, and time. Publishing, scheduling, editing live posts, or replying externally requires explicit authorization unless covered by a written pre-approved scope. Never assume connector availability; if no publishing tool exists, return an import-ready calendar.

## Engagement and learning

Draft helpful routine replies that reflect the author's voice. Escalate complaints, factual disputes, harassment, legal/privacy issues, sensitive topics, refund requests, and hostile reviews. Never impersonate an independent reader or conceal promotional intent.

Evaluate qualified clicks, landing/store conversion, signups, sales, cost, and assisted effects before engagement or reach. Compare posts by objective and audience, not across unlike formats. Repurpose winners only after identifying the likely mechanism.

Return ready-to-use copy, channel adaptations, creative briefs, a calendar or payload, tracking fields, approval state, and the measurement/iteration rule.
