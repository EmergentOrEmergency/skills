# Ads Growth

Treat paid media as a controlled experiment, not a default growth engine. Confirm that the storefront or landing page is credible and measurable before buying more traffic.

## Preflight

Establish platform/account, territory, format, price, net contribution per sale, conversion destination, attribution window, historical baseline, total and daily budget caps, acceptable loss for learning, target CAC/ROAS, prohibited targeting, and approval scope. Verify current platform capability and policy from official documentation.

No authorization is implied by a budget mentioned in a brief. Creating, launching, increasing spend, broadening targeting, or making irreversible campaign changes requires explicit approval unless the user has given a written pre-authorized envelope with amount, duration, accounts, and stop rules.

## Experiment design

Change one major variable when causal learning matters. Define hypothesis, audience or keyword, offer, creative, placement, bid strategy, budget, duration, primary metric, guardrails, minimum evidence, and stop/scale rule before launch. Use campaign naming and UTM conventions that link to the experiment ledger.

Avoid audiences or claims that violate policy, sensitive-trait inference, misleading creative, forced clicks, fabricated endorsements, or competitor trademark misuse. Do not infer reader identity from protected or sensitive data.

## Optimization

Separate delivery, click, landing/store conversion, attributed sales, and contribution profit. Reconcile platform-reported conversions with sales data and disclose attribution overlap. Do not scale on CTR alone or overreact to tiny samples.

Recommend:

- `STOP` when a pre-agreed hard guardrail is crossed;
- `PAUSE_FOR_REVIEW` when data integrity, policy, tracking, or reputation is at risk;
- `CONTINUE` when evidence is insufficient but the test remains within its loss budget;
- `ITERATE` when the mechanism is plausible and one variable should change;
- `SCALE` only when profitability or strategic learning clears the defined threshold.

Return campaign architecture, variants, budget table, exact approval request, measurement plan, and a decision log. When connected tools are unavailable or access is unapproved, produce import-ready settings rather than claiming execution.
