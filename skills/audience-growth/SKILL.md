# Audience Growth

Grow a relevant, reachable reader base rather than raw follower counts. Optimize for qualified attention, consented contact, trust, repeat engagement, referrals, and purchase intent.

For short stories and serial fiction, use `fiction-discovery` when available to choose the entry story, release order, and publication route. Build the subscriber/community journey around its reading promise and schedule; track next-story clicks separately from confirmed return reading.

## Map the audience system

Define primary and secondary reader segments by situation, motivation, desired experience, objections, comparable affinities, discovery behavior, and preferred channel. Avoid invented personas with unsupported demographic detail.

Map the journey: `discovery -> proof/relevance -> sample or deeper engagement -> purchase -> read -> review/referral -> next book`.

Identify the current bottleneck and the smallest useful asset or relationship to address it: landing page, sample, reader magnet, newsletter sequence, community contribution, partner bundle, podcast pitch, reviewer kit, ARC process, or referral loop.

## Outreach and communities

Research fit before contact. Personalize from genuine relevance, disclose the relationship and any compensation or free copy, provide an easy opt-out, and keep a suppression/contact ledger. Never purchase reviews, condition a copy on positive coverage, mass-message scraped contacts, fabricate familiarity, or automate participation that pretends to be a human reader.

Community participation must provide standalone value and follow local rules. Drafting is autonomous; sending, posting, mass outreach, or contacting high-profile people requires the user's authorization unless an explicit pre-approved operating envelope exists.

## Experiments and measurement

Define a primary behavioral metric appropriate to the stage: qualified signup rate, sample consumption, reply rate, retailer click-through, attributed purchase, referral, or retained subscriber. Track quality and cost alongside volume. Use engagement only as a diagnostic.

For each experiment record hypothesis, segment, message or offer, channel, consent basis, cost, duration, success threshold, stop rule, outcome, and learning. Prefer small batches that allow message revision before scaling.

## Deliverable

Provide segment priority, channel-role map, journey bottleneck, 30-day growth loop, asset backlog, outreach drafts or approval-ready batches, measurement plan, and explicit do-not-cross boundaries.
